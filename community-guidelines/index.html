Community Guidelines

CoachCupid LLC  ·  Effective July 12, 2026  ·  Last updated July 12, 2026

Welcome to CoachCupid.


CoachCupid exists so that real people can get honest, constructive feedback on their dating profiles — and give it. That only works when everyone shows up with honesty, kindness, and respect.


These Community Guidelines set expectations for how we treat each other here. They apply to everything you do on CoachCupid — your profile, your reviews, your captions, and how you interact with other users. Not following them has real consequences, from a warning to a permanent ban.


Read on. This matters.

CoachCupid's Rules

1 Be yourself.

CoachCupid is built on authenticity. The feedback you get is only useful if what you're showing is real. That means your photos should actually look like you, your prompts should reflect who you actually are, and your profile should represent your genuine self — not an idealized or misleading version of it.

Don't impersonate anyone else or create a fake account. One account per person.

2 Give feedback that actually helps.

The whole point of CoachCupid is constructive feedback. That means feedback that's honest, specific, and genuinely useful to the person receiving it. Think about what you'd want to hear if it were your profile.

Good feedback looks like:

  • "Your first photo doesn't show your face clearly — try leading with one that does."
  • "This prompt answer is vague — what do you actually mean by that?"
  • "Great energy in your photos, but the lighting in photo 3 is working against you."

Bad feedback looks like:

  • Personal attacks or insults
  • Comments about someone's body, weight, or physical appearance in a demeaning way
  • Mocking, sarcastic, or cruel remarks
  • One-word responses that provide no actual value ("bad," "ugly," "boring")
  • Sexual comments or advances
  • Anything you wouldn't be comfortable saying to someone's face

Reviews that violate these guidelines will be removed, and repeated violations will result in account suspension.

3 Respect boundaries.

CoachCupid is a feedback platform, not a dating app. Reviewers are here to help submitters improve their profiles — not to hit on them, pursue them romantically, or make unwanted advances.

Not allowed:

  • Using reviews to make romantic or sexual advances toward submitters
  • Asking submitters for their contact information, social handles, or to connect outside the app
  • Sending harassing, threatening, or intimidating messages
  • Continuing to engage with a user after they've made clear they don't want contact
4 No hate speech or discrimination.

CoachCupid is for everyone. We don't tolerate hate speech, slurs, harmful stereotypes, or discriminatory language of any kind — in reviews, prompts, captions, or anywhere else on the platform.

This includes discrimination based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, immigration status, or any other characteristic.

If someone's profile doesn't match your preferences, that's okay — just move on. Don't leave hateful or discriminatory feedback.

5 No body shaming.

Comments about someone's body, weight, size, or physical appearance in a negative or demeaning way are not allowed on CoachCupid — ever. This applies to reviews, captions, and any other content on the platform.

There's a meaningful difference between constructive feedback ("the lighting in this photo isn't flattering") and body shaming ("you're overweight"). The first helps someone. The second just hurts them.

If your feedback is about someone's body rather than their profile, it doesn't belong here.

6 Adults only.

You must be 18 years of age or older to use CoachCupid. Any account found to belong to someone under 18 will be immediately and permanently removed.

Do not post photos that include minors — including childhood photos of yourself. Do not share, solicit, or reference any content that involves minors in a sexual or inappropriate context. Violations will result in an immediate permanent ban and may be reported to law enforcement.

7 No harmful or violent content.

CoachCupid is a positive space. We don't allow content that is violent, graphic, or harmful — in profiles, photos, reviews, or anywhere else.

Not allowed:

  • Images or descriptions of violence, gore, or injury
  • Content that glorifies or encourages self-harm or suicide
  • Content promoting dangerous activities or extremist ideologies
  • Threats of any kind toward other users or CoachCupid staff
  • Nudity or sexually explicit content

If you're going through a difficult time and need support, please reach out to a trusted person or a crisis resource in your area.

8 Keep it personal, not commercial.

CoachCupid is for getting better at dating — not for advertising, selling, or promoting. Don't use the platform to market products, services, or yourself in a commercial capacity.

Not allowed:

  • Promoting products, services, brands, or social media accounts
  • Soliciting money, donations, or investments from other users
  • Spamming or sending unsolicited promotional messages
  • Using CoachCupid to recruit clients, customers, or followers
9 Follow the law.

Don't use CoachCupid for anything illegal. This includes using the platform to buy, sell, or distribute illegal substances, facilitating fraud, or engaging in any other unlawful activity. Illegal content will be removed and reported to the appropriate authorities where required by law.

10 One person, one account.

Each person may only have one CoachCupid account. Creating multiple accounts — especially to evade a ban — is not permitted. If your account is suspended or terminated, you may not create a new one without our express written permission.

Reporting

If you see content or behavior that violates these guidelines, please report it. You can flag any review or submission directly in the app. Your report is confidential — we will never share your identity with the person you reported.

We take all reports seriously. Submitting false or malicious reports is itself a violation of these guidelines and may result in account action.

You can also contact us directly at maceweisman@gmail.com.

Consequences

What happens if you break the rules?

Violations of these Community Guidelines may result in:


• Content removal — the offending review, photo, or prompt is deleted
• A warning — for less serious or first-time violations
• Temporary suspension — for more serious or repeated violations
• Permanent ban — for severe violations or patterns of abuse


Certain violations — including hate speech, threats, content involving minors, or illegal activity — will result in an immediate permanent ban with no prior warning.


If you believe your account was actioned in error, contact us at maceweisman@gmail.com and we will review your case.

Remember: These guidelines exist to make CoachCupid better for everyone. We're building something genuinely useful — a community where people feel safe getting honest feedback on something as personal as their dating profile. That only works if we all take care of each other.

Be kind. Be honest. Be constructive. That's the CoachCupid way.
