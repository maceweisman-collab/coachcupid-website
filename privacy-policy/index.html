6. Advertising

We run advertising campaigns to promote CoachCupid on platforms including Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok, Google, YouTube, and Apple Search Ads. Here's what that means for your data:

What we may share with ad platforms: We may share limited data — such as a hashed (anonymized) version of your email address or device advertising identifiers — with advertising platforms to measure how effective our ads are, and to reach new users who are similar to our existing community. This is sometimes called "custom audience" or "lookalike" advertising.

Tracking technologies: We may use advertising SDKs or pixels provided by these platforms within the app. These tools help us understand whether someone who saw our ad went on to install CoachCupid. The platforms involved may include:

  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — Privacy Policy
  • TikTok — Privacy Policy
  • Google / YouTube — Privacy Policy
  • Apple (Search Ads) — Privacy Policy

Your choices: You can limit ad tracking at the device level:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → opt out of ads personalization

We do not use your dating profile content (photos, prompts, reviews, sexual orientation, or ethnicity) for advertising purposes. Advertising data is limited to account-level and technical identifiers only.

US residents: Some of the activities described in this section may constitute "targeted advertising" or "sharing" under applicable US state laws. You have the right to opt out — see Section 8 for details.

9. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your data only as long as necessary to provide the service and comply with our legal obligations:

  • Active account data: retained for the duration of your account
  • After account deletion: most data is deleted within 30 days. We retain limited data for up to 90 days to protect the safety and security of our community (for example, to investigate reports of abuse)
  • Purchase records: retained for up to 7 years to comply with tax and accounting requirements
  • Legal holds: if we receive a valid legal request to preserve data, we will retain it for as long as required
  • Anonymized/aggregated data: may be retained indefinitely as it no longer identifies you

10. Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated personal data in two ways:

  • In-app: Navigate to Profile → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, photos, prompts, reviews, Feedback Points, and all personal data.
  • By email: Contact us at maceweisman@gmail.com and we will process your request promptly.

Some data may be retained for the limited periods described in Section 9 above where required by law or for safety purposes.

11. Children's Privacy

CoachCupid is strictly for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. All users must confirm they are 18 or older during registration.

If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, please contact us immediately at maceweisman@gmail.com and we will delete the account and associated data promptly.

12. Security

We take the security of your data seriously, especially given the sensitive nature of dating profile information. We implement technical and organizational measures including encrypted data transmission, secure cloud storage via Supabase, and access controls that limit who can view your data.

That said, no system is completely impenetrable. In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours and inform affected users without undue delay, as required by applicable law.

13. Privacy Policy Changes
