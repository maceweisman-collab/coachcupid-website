Child Safety Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Our Commitment

CoachCupid LLC is firmly committed to the safety and protection of minors. We have a zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form on our platform.

Age Requirements

CoachCupid is intended solely for users who are 18 years of age or older. We require all users to confirm they are 18 or older during account registration. Users found to be under 18 will have their accounts immediately terminated.

Prohibited Content

The following content is strictly prohibited on CoachCupid:

  • Any sexual content involving minors
  • Any content that exploits, abuses, or endangers children
  • Any content that sexualizes individuals under 18
  • Any grooming behavior or attempts to solicit minors

Enforcement Measures

CoachCupid enforces these standards through:

  • Age confirmation requirement during registration
  • User reporting system for flagging inappropriate content
  • Active moderation and review of flagged content
  • Immediate account termination for violations
  • Cooperation with law enforcement when required by law

Reporting

If you encounter any content that violates our child safety policy, please report it immediately through the app's flag/report feature or contact us directly at eli@coachcupid.app.

We report all confirmed instances of child sexual abuse material (CSAM) to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) as required by law.
